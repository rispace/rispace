## About

I'm a mathematician working at the intersection of **probability theory**, **statistical sampling**, and **machine learning**. My research focuses on designing and analyzing efficient algorithms for sampling from complex distributions — particularly in high-dimensional and constrained settings.

*RISPACE* is a nod to the mathematical spaces — Euclidean, Hilbert, Banach — that underpin the work I do every day.


> *"Sampling is the bridge between the theoretical and the computable."*
